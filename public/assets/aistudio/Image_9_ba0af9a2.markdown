## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, high-clarity enterprise aesthetic engineered for cross-cultural business diplomacy and executive AI decision-making. The personality communicates three fundamental virtues: **Global Gravitas**, **Precision Intelligence**, and **Empathetic Nuance**. It serves enterprise leaders, cross-border negotiators, and global sales teams who need reliable cultural protocol guidance without gimmicks.

The design movement combines **Modern Corporate Minimalism** with the utilitarian precision of developer-first enterprise tools (Linear, Stripe, Vercel). The aesthetic strictly rejects nebulous AI gradients, rainbow mesh blurs, and neon glow effects ("no AI slop"). Instead, structure is achieved through crisp 1px borders, pristine slate backgrounds, rich optical typography, high information density, and deliberate spatial organization.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative deep darks with high-efficiency structural neutrals and strategic functional accents:

- **Primary (`#4F46E5` / `#6366F1`):** Royal Indigo. Used for focused actions, active navigation states, primary buttons, and key interactive focal points.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A` / `#1E1B4B`):** Deep Navy & Midnight Obsidian. Anchors structural sidebars, high-contrast badges, deep card footers, and primary typography.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#D97706`):** Warm Cultural Amber. Reserved exclusively for cultural etiquette alerts, context callouts, protocol warnings, and high-value insights.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces (`#F8FAFC`, `#FFFFFF`, `#F1F5F9`, `#E2E8F0`, `#334155`):** Clean slate foundation. Canvas sits on `#F8FAFC`, elevated panels and cards on `#FFFFFF`, structural section borders on `#E2E8F0`, and secondary text on `#334155` and `#64748B`.

### Semantic Tokens
- **Success:** `#10B981` (Emerald) — Cross-cultural alignment, protocol confirmed, verified etiquette.
- **Warning:** `#F59E0B` (Amber) — Cultural sensitivity warning, indirect communication notice.
- **Critical:** `#EF4444` (Crimson) — Taboo violation, communication risk, deal-breaker etiquette breach.
- **Info / AI Context:** `#4F46E5` (Indigo) — Synthesized cultural copilot suggestions.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural display and section headers with **Inter** for dense, readable tabular data, negotiation scripts, and email previews.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Provides modern geometric structure with subtle humanist warmth, ensuring headlines convey authority without feeling cold.
- **Inter:** Chosen for body copy and metadata due to its exceptional micro-legibility at 12px–14px sizes, tall x-height, and neutral character in dense multi-column enterprise layouts.
- **Numerics & Scenarios:** Tabular figures are enabled across all data summaries, country metric comparison cards, and analytical indexes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored by a persistent navigation rail (240px desktop, collapsible to 64px compact icon rail) and a 12-column responsive fluid content canvas with fixed inner constraints (`max-width: 1440px`).

### Spatial Hierarchy
- **Canvas Margins:** `2rem` (32px) on desktop viewports (>1024px); `1rem` (16px) on mobile viewports (<768px).
- **Grid Gutters:** Consistent `1.5rem` (24px) gutters between analytical cards and widget modules.
- **Card Padding Rhythms:** Internal card padding is strictly uniform at `1.5rem` (24px) for hero and primary tools, down to `1rem` (16px) for compact metric cards.
- **Data Density:** Visual noise is reduced by matching label-to-input gap at `0.375rem` (6px) and input-to-input stack spacing at `1rem` (16px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual separation relies on crisp border definitions combined with micro-level ambient drop shadows rather than blurred elevation layers:

- **Base Layer:** Canvas sits at `#F8FAFC`.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Form Containers):** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, shadow `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdown Menus, Modals, Overlays):** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, shadow `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Active Navigation Pill:** Tinted indigo surface (`#4F46E5`), sharp contrast, no drop-glow.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Subtle, architectural 1px lines using `#E2E8F0` on light containers and `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` on deep navy sidebars.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on a balanced **Rounded (`roundedness: 2`)** geometry that feels modern, engineered, and executive:

- **Core Components (Inputs, Buttons, Feature Badges):** `0.5rem` (8px) to `0.75rem` (12px / `rounded-xl`).
- **Cards, Modals, and Dashboard Widgets:** Standardized at `0.75rem` (12px / `rounded-xl`) to maintain crisp architectural cohesion.
- **Pills & Status Tags:** Fully rounded (`9999px`) reserved specifically for etiquette status chips, country markers, and category indicator tags.
- **Hero & Interactive Assistant Banners:** `1rem` (16px) corner radius.

- Interactive action cards include subtle transition on border color (`#CBD5E1`) and directional action arrow circular buttons (`#EEF2FF` to `#4F46E5` on hover).

### Checkboxes & Toggle Radios

### Cultural Context Banners
- Subtle linear background tinting from `#EFF6FF` to `#FFFFFF`, framed with `1px solid #DBEAFE`. Paired with a cultural lightbulb icon (`#4F46E5`) and contextual executive summaries.
